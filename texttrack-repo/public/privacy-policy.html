Privacy Policy

How Chipperfield Ag handles your information across our platforms, including TextTrack.

TextTrack SMS Disclosure By participating in TextTrack, you may receive text messages related to your training. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. No mobile information is shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving texts.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TextTrack ("TextTrack," "the Platform," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Chipperfield Ag Erectors LLC ("the Company"), collects, uses, stores, and protects information in connection with the TextTrack employee training platform, including its SMS/MMS text message delivery, employee-facing web application, and administrator dashboard.

1. Scope — Training Purposes Only

TextTrack is an internal employee training and development tool. It exists solely to deliver job-related training content to employees of the Company and to allow authorized administrators and managers to monitor training progress and engagement. TextTrack is not a marketing platform, a recruiting tool, a customer communication channel, or a general-purpose messaging service, and it will not be used to send promotional, political, or unrelated commercial content of any kind.

Access to TextTrack is limited to current employees who have been enrolled by an authorized administrator, and to administrators/managers who have been granted access for the purpose of managing training programs.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact and profile information: your name, mobile phone number, job title/role, work team or department, and employer/organization.
  • Enrollment information: which training track(s) you are assigned to, your start date, and any schedule adjustments.
  • Message delivery data: records of which training messages were sent to you and when, delivery status (delivered, failed, pending), and the content of messages sent.
  • Engagement data: whether and when you tap a link included in a training message, your current position within a training track, completion status, and (if applicable) your device type and general engagement timing. We may log the IP address associated with a link click and coarse device information strictly to confirm engagement and troubleshoot delivery issues.
  • Inbound messages: if you reply to a TextTrack message (including STOP, START, HELP, or any other reply), we record that reply and the time it was received.
  • Administrator-entered information: internal notes or comments a manager or administrator may add to your training record for coaching or program-management purposes.

We do not collect precise GPS location, browsing history outside the Platform, biometric data, or any information about your family, health, finances, or activities outside of your participation in TextTrack.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above exclusively to operate and improve the training program, specifically to:

  • Send you scheduled training messages and links to training content relevant to your assigned track(s);
  • Track your progress through your assigned training so that you and your managers can see where you stand;
  • Generate reports and summaries for administrators and managers to support coaching conversations and program oversight;
  • Identify and troubleshoot delivery problems;
  • Process opt-out (STOP) and opt-back-in (START) requests, and respond to HELP requests;
  • Maintain the security, integrity, and reliability of the Platform;
  • Improve training content over time based on aggregate, not individually targeted, engagement patterns.

We do not use your information to make automated employment decisions (such as hiring, termination, or discipline) without human review, and training engagement data is intended to support coaching, not to serve as the sole basis for any adverse employment action.

4. What We Do Not Do With Your Information

Our commitment

Your information collected through TextTrack is used for training purposes only. We do not:

  • Sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number or any other personal information to any third party;
  • Share your mobile number with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes, under any circumstances;
  • Use your mobile number or contact information for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to this training program;
  • Share your consent to receive text messages with any third party for their own marketing purposes;
  • Disclose your individual training data outside the Company except as described in Section 6 below.

5. SMS/MMS Messaging Terms

You can enroll in TextTrack either by completing the sign-up form at training.chipperfield.ag/enroll-preview.html, or by texting JOIN to our TextTrack number. By enrolling — through either method — you consent to receive recurring automated text messages (SMS/MMS) related to your assigned training program at the mobile phone number you provide.

Mobile Number & Messaging Disclosures

No mobile information — including phone numbers and any related personal information — will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. This information is used solely to deliver and support your assigned training program.

Message frequency varies by training track and schedule. You may receive multiple messages per week depending on the pace of your assigned training, plus occasional system notices or alerts.

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

  • Consent to receive messages is captured and time-stamped at the time of enrollment and is not a condition of employment beyond what is required to complete assigned training through the format designated by the Company.
  • You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message. Opting out will immediately stop delivery of further text messages; your manager will be notified so that alternative arrangements for completing required training can be made.
  • You may resume text messages at any time by replying START.
  • Reply HELP at any time for assistance, or contact jessica@chipperfield.ag directly.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

6. How We Share Information

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within the Company: your training progress, engagement data, and profile information are visible to authorized administrators and your direct manager(s) for the purpose of program oversight and coaching.
  • Service providers: we use third-party infrastructure providers — including our SMS/MMS delivery provider (Twilio) and our cloud hosting providers — solely to operate the Platform. These providers process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations, and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required to do so by law, subpoena, or valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the Company or its employees.
  • Business changes: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, training records may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to the same protections described in this Policy.

We do not share your individual training data with any external client, vendor, or the general public.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable, industry-standard measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of personal information (including phone numbers and profile data) at rest in our database and in transit between systems;
  • Role-based access controls, so administrators and managers can only see data relevant to their team or responsibility;
  • Multi-factor authentication for administrator accounts;
  • Audit logging of administrator actions that view or modify employee records;
  • Regular review of who has access to the Platform, with access removed promptly when no longer needed.

No system can guarantee perfect security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected individuals and any relevant authorities as required by applicable law.

8. Data Retention

We retain training records for as long as needed to support ongoing training programs and for a reasonable period afterward for recordkeeping purposes. When an employee separates from the Company, their TextTrack data will be handled as follows:

  • Active messaging will stop immediately upon separation;
  • Historical training completion records may be retained as part of standard personnel recordkeeping, consistent with the Company's general employee records retention practices;
  • Phone numbers and other contact details not needed for recordkeeping will be removed from active messaging systems within 90 days of separation.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Review the personal information TextTrack holds about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate profile information (e.g., an updated phone number);
  • Opt out of text messages at any time (see Section 5);
  • Ask questions about how your training data is used, by contacting your manager or jessica@chipperfield.ag.

Because TextTrack is a job-related training tool, some records (such as a historical completion record) may be retained as part of standard employment recordkeeping even after an opt-out, consistent with Section 8.

10. Children's Privacy

TextTrack is intended solely for use by employees of the Company and is not directed at, or knowingly used by, individuals under the age of 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the Platform or applicable law. If we make material changes, we will notify enrolled employees through the Platform or via a training message. Continued use of TextTrack after such notice constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.
